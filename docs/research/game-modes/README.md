## Current Design Context

The project is not assumed to be a match that happens to have a lobby. A
plausible direction is a multiplayer place containing several levels of
commitment:

1. A lounge or commons for social presence, movement practice, spectating, and
   team entry.
2. Open activities that work solo or at low population and allow voluntary
   competition.
3. Formal competitive sets with locked teams, bounded rules, and recorded
   results.

Current research therefore values:

- Skiing as a primary game verb rather than transit between gunfights.
- Solo, 1v1, and uneven-population behavior.
- Opt-in intensity and legitimate downtime.
- Chasing, route prediction, interception, and recoverable mistakes.
- Objectives that remain readable at 30-80 m/s.
- Rules that reuse server-owned movement, combat, gates, flags, and score.
- Maps that support several experiments through reusable sockets and routes.

## Lifecycle Snapshot

No mode is adopted by this table.

| Concept | Lifecycle | Immediate question |
|---|---|---|
| Contested Core | Explore | Does target unlocking create a satisfying convergence before a readable ski pursuit? |
| Open CTF | Seed | Can endless flag play recreate low-pop route practice and voluntary defense without losing purpose? |
| Breakline | Seed | Can route penetration become approachable when urgency is opt-in rather than constant? |
| Foxhunt | Seed | Can periodic information create a good 1v1 pursuit game? |
| Linecraft | Seed | Can players set and share movement challenges without requiring a full editor? |
| Courier / Relay Grid | Seed | Can ambient deliveries turn an open basin into casual activity and emergent competition? |
| Lounge / Commons | Seed | Can players enter, observe, practice, and leave active play without fragmenting a small population? |

## Research Rules

- Record variants before selecting one from imagination alone.
- Test the disputed verb before building the complete mode.
- Prefer wide traversal volumes over tiny precision triggers.
- Prefer spatial counterplay and short resets over rule exceptions.
- Do not add capable bots merely to hide a population dependency.
- Do not use final art to rescue an unclear loop.
- Keep objectives, ownership, crossings, timers, and score authoritative.
- Preserve failed forms and the evidence that failed them.
