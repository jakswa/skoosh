# SKOOSH Exploratory Research

This area preserves exploratory product and game-design research. It is not
current project truth, an implementation roadmap, or an adopted decision.

Read [`../CHECKPOINT.md`](../CHECKPOINT.md) first. When these notes disagree
with the current implementation, checkpoint, active owning documents, or
decision records, those sources win.

## Research Areas

| Area | Purpose |
|---|---|
| [Game directions and modes](game-modes/README.md) | Candidate player fantasies, rule loops, genre combinations, and cheap experiments. |
| [Engineering lineage](engineering-lineage/README.md) | Durable Torque3D, OpenTNL, and Tribes architecture and feel lessons. |

Engineering lineage research asks what historical architecture and feel lessons
can be carried forward cleanly. Game-mode research asks what players might do
with SKOOSH once the movement and networking substrate is trustworthy.

## Authority And Adoption

- Research entries preserve possibilities, including weak and expensive ones.
- A lifecycle label records evidence and attention, not intrinsic quality.
- `Defer` and `Reject` entries remain available as mechanic donors.
- Human play is required before a concept advances beyond `Prototype`.
- An adopted result must move into the relevant active document or decision
  record. Do not quietly turn research into a second checkpoint.
